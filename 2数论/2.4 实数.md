# 1 实数：由有理数扩充至实数
- ==定义== **实数**：
	- 实数定义为形如有理柯西序列极限，记作$LIM(a_{n})$。每一个实数$LIM(a_{n})$与有理柯西序列$a_{n}$对应，每一个有理柯西序列$a_{n}$与实数$LIM(a_{n})$对应。实数集记作$R$。

- ==定义== **实数的相等**：
	- 如果两个实数$$LIM(a_{n})=LIM(b_{n})\Leftrightarrow a_{n},~b_{n}是等价的有理柯西序列$$

# 2 实数有序域：实数基本代数结构
- ==定义== **实数的加法**：
	- 定义实数$LIM(a_{n}),~LIM(b_{n})$的**加法**为$$LIM(a_{n})+LIM(b_{n})=LIM(a_{n}+b_{n})$$
		- 加法替换原则（加法良定义）和加法消去律$$LIM(a_{n})=LIM(a'_{n})\Leftrightarrow LIM(a_{n})+LIM(b_{n})=LIM(a'_{n})+LIM(b_{n})$$
		>$\forall\varepsilon>0,~\exists N,~\forall n\geq N,~d(a_{n}+b_{n},~a'_{n}+b_{n})=|~(a_{n}+b_{n})-(a'_{n}+b_{n})~|=|~a_{n}-a'_{n}~|=d(a_{n},~a'_{n})<\varepsilon$
	- 定义实数$LIM(a_{n})$的**相反数**为$$x=-LIM(a_{n})\Leftrightarrow方程LIM(a_{n})+x=LIM(0_{n})的解$$
		- 方程的解为$x=LIM(-a_{n})$$$-LIM(a_{n})=(-1)_{n}*LIM(a_{n})=LIM(-a_{n})$$
		>$LIM(a_{n})+LIM(-a_{n})=LIM(a_{n}+(-a_{n}))=LIM(0_{n})\implies方程有解LIM(-a_{n})$
		>$LIM(a_{n})+LIM(b_{n})=LIM(a_{n})+LIM(c_{n})=LIM(0_{n})\implies方程有唯一解$
	- 定义实数$LIM(a_{n}),~LIM(b_{n})$的**减法**为$$x=LIM(b_{n})-LIM(a_{n})\Leftrightarrow方程LIM(a_{n})+x=LIM(b_{n})的解$$
		- 方程的解为$x=LIM(b_{n}-a_{n})$，称作**位移数**。$$LIM(b_{n})-LIM(a_{n})=LIM(b_{n})+(-LIM(a_{n}))=LIM(b_{n})+(-1)*LIM(a_{n})=LIM(b_{n})+LIM(-a_{n})=LIM(b_{n}-a_{n})$$
		>$方程有解LIM(b_{n}-a_{n})$
		>$方程有唯一解$

- ==定义== **实数的乘法**：
	- 定义实数$LIM(a_{n}),~LIM(b_{n})$的**乘法**为$$LIM(a_{n})*LIM(b_{n})=LIM(a_{n}*b_{n})$$
		- 乘法替换原则与乘法消去律$$LIM(a_{n})=LIM(a'_{n})\Leftrightarrow LIM(a_{n})*LIM(b_{n})=LIM(a'_{n})*LIM(b_{n})$$
		>$\forall\varepsilon>0,~\exists N,~\forall n\geq N,~d(a_{n},~a'_{n})=|~a_{n}-a'_{n}~|\leq\varepsilon\implies d(a_{n}b_{n},~a'_{n}b_{n})=|~(a_{n}-a'_{n})b_{n}~|\leq |~b_{n}~|d(a_{n},~a'_{n})\leq M\varepsilon$
	- 定义不与$0_{n}$等价的实数$LIM(a_{n})$的**倒数**为$$x=LIM(a_{n})'\Leftrightarrow方程LIM(a_{n})*x=1的解$$
		- $方程的解为x=LIM\left( \frac{1}{a'_{n}} \right)$，其中$a'_{n}$是与$a_{n}$等价的非零有理序列$$LIM(a_{n})'=LIM(a'^{-1}_{n})=LIM\left( \frac{1}{a' _{n}} \right)$$
		>$方程有解LIM\left( \frac{1}{a'_{n}} \right)$
		>$方程有唯一解$
	- 定义实数$LIM(a_{n}),~LIM(b_{n})$的**除法**为$$x=LIM(a_{n})/LIM(b_{n})\Leftrightarrow方程LIM(a_{n})*x=LIM(b_{n})的解$$
		- $方程的解为x=LIM\left( \frac{b_{n}}{a_{n}} \right)$，称作**比例数**。$$LIM(b_{n})/LIM(a_{n})=LIM(b_{n})*LIM(a_{n})'=LIM(b_{n})*LIM\left( \frac{1}{a_{n}} \right)=LIM\left( \frac{b_{n}}{a_{n}} \right)$$
		>$方程有解LIM\left( \frac{b_{n}}{a_{n}} \right)$
		>$方程有唯一解$

- ==定理== **实数域**：基本运算的性质
	- $LIM(a_{n})+LIM(b_{n})=LIM(b_{n})+LIM(a_{n})$
	- $(LIM(a_{n})+LIM(b_{n}))+LIM(c_{n})=LIM(a_{n})+(LIM(b_{n})+LIM(c_{n}))$
	- $LIM(a_{n})*LIM(b_{n})=LIM(b_{n})*LIM(a_{n})$
	- $(LIM(a_{n})*LIM(b_{n}))*LIM(c_{n})=LIM(a_{n})*(LIM(b_{n})*LIM(c_{n}))$
	- $LIM(a_{n})*(LIM(b_{n})+LIM(c_{n}))=LIM(a_{n})*LIM(b_{n})+LIM(a_{n})*LIM(c_{n})$
	- $LIM(a_{n})+LIM(0)=LIM(a_{n})$
	- $LIM(a_{n})*LIM(1)=LIM(a_{n})$
	- $LIM(a_{n})+(-LIM(a_{n}))=LIM(0)$
	- $LIM(a_{n})*LIM(a_{n})'=LIM(1)$
	>$略$

- ==定义== **实数序**：实数的大小比较
	- 定义实数（实数依$LIM(0)$划分正负）$$LIM(a_{n})是正数\Leftrightarrow \exists\varepsilon>0,~\exists N,~\forall n\geq N,~a_{n}\geq\varepsilon\text{（最终正远离0）}$$$$LIM(a_{n})是负数\Leftrightarrow\exists\varepsilon>0,~\exists N,~\forall n\geq N,~a_{n}\leq-\varepsilon\text{（最终负远离0）}$$
		- 任意实数要么是正实数、要么是负实数、要么为$LIM(0)$
		>$对任意有理柯西序列，由等价关系，要么与LIM(0)等价，要么不与LIM(0)等价$
		>$对任意不与LIM(0)等价的柯西序列，\exists \varepsilon'>0,~\forall N,~\exists n\geq N,~|~a_{n}~|\geq\varepsilon' \land \forall\varepsilon>0,~\exists N,~\forall m,~n\geq N,~|~a_{n}-a_{m}~|\leq\varepsilon$
		>$\implies \exists\varepsilon'>0,~\exists N_{1},~\exists n_{0}\geq N_{1},~|~a_{n_{0}}~|\geq\varepsilon'$
		>$若a_{n_{0}}是正有理数，\exists\varepsilon=\frac{\varepsilon'}{2},~\exists N_{2},~\forall m,~n\geq N_{2},~|~a_{n}-a_{m}~|\leq \frac{\varepsilon'}{2}.取N=max\{ N_{1},~N_{2} \},~n_{0}\geq N,~\forall m\geq N,~|~a_{m}-a_{n_{0}}~|\leq \frac{\varepsilon'}{2}$
		>$\implies \exists \frac{\varepsilon'}{2}>0,~\exists N,~\forall m\geq N,~a_{m}\geq a_{n_{0}}- \frac{\varepsilon'}{2}\geq\varepsilon'- \frac{\varepsilon'}{2}= \frac{\varepsilon'}{2}\implies正实数$
		>$同理令a_{n_{0}}为负有理数。$
		>$若实数既正又负\implies \varepsilon\leq a_{n}\leq -\varepsilon当且仅当\varepsilon=0$
		- 正实数的相反数是负实数，负实数的相反数是正实数，0的相反数为0。
		>$若a_{n}\geq\varepsilon\implies-a_{n}\leq -\varepsilon\implies LIM(-a_{n})是负实数$
		>$若a_{n}\leq-\varepsilon \implies -a_{n}\geq\varepsilon\implies LIM(-a_{n})是正实数$
		>$0+0=0$
		- 正实数的和、积、倒数均是正实数
		>$略$
	- 定义实数上的小于等于关系$$a\leq b\Leftrightarrow\exists k是非负实数，a+k=b$$定义实数上的小于关系$$a<b\Leftrightarrow a\leq b\land a\neq b\Leftrightarrow\exists k是正实数，a+k=b$$
		- 与0的比较$$a是正实数\Leftrightarrow a>0$$$$a是负实数\Leftrightarrow a<0$$
		>$\exists k=a,~0+a=a$
		>$\exists k=-a是正实数，a+(-a)=0$
		- 比较大小方式
			- 比较位移数$$a\leq b\Leftrightarrow a-b\leq0$$
			- 比较比例数$$\begin{cases}a\leq b\Leftrightarrow \frac{a}{b}\leq1 & a,~b同正 \\a\leq b\Leftrightarrow \frac{a}{b}\geq1 & a,~b同负\\负数\leq0\leq正数&a,~b异号或a=0\end{cases}$$
			- 根据定义比较有理数$$\exists N,~\forall n\geq N,~a_{n}\leq b_{n}\implies LIM(a_{n})\leq LIM(b_{n})$$
		>$略$
		- 任意小定理$$a\leq b\Leftrightarrow\forall\varepsilon> 0,~a<b+\varepsilon$$$$b<a\Leftrightarrow \exists\varepsilon>0,~a\geq b+\varepsilon$$
		>$略$

- ==定理== **实数有序域**：比较的性质

- ==定理== **有理数扩充**：实数子集与有理数同构

- ==定理== **实数散布**：阿基米德性
	- 正实数可以任意大也可以任意小$$\forall a>0,~\exists N_{1}\in N^{+},~a> \frac{1}{N_{1}},~\exists N_{2},~a<N_{2}$$
	>$a>0 \implies \exists \varepsilon>0,~\exists N,~\forall n\geq N,~a_{n}\geq\varepsilon>0\implies \exists \frac{p}{q}\in Q,~a_{n}\geq\varepsilon> \frac{p}{q}\geq \frac{1}{q}>0\implies \exists N_{1}=q+1,~a\geq \frac{1}{q}> \frac{1}{q+1}$
	>$\exists M,~\exists N,~\forall n\geq N,~a_{n}\leq M\implies \exists r\in N,~a_{n}\leq M<r\implies \exists N_{2}=r,~a<r$
	- 阿基米德性$$\forall b>a>0,~\exists N,~Na>b$$
	>$\exists N,~N> \frac{b}{a}\implies Na>b$
	- 整数对实数的界定，其中$N$记作为$a$的向下取整数$N=[a]$。$$\forall a\in R,~!\exists N\in Z,~N\leq a<N+1$$
	>证明一：如何找
	>$\forall \varepsilon>0,~\exists N,~\forall m,~n\geq N,~|~a_{n}-a_{m}~|<\varepsilon$
	>$\implies 取\varepsilon=\frac{1}{2},~M\geq N,~|~a_{n}-a_{M}~|< \frac{1}{2},~\exists M',~M'\leq a_{M}<M'+1\implies M'-\frac{1}{2}\leq a_{M}-\frac{1}{2}<a_{n}<a_{M}+ \frac{1}{2}<M'+1+\frac{1}{2}$
	>$\implies M'-\frac{1}{2}<a<M'+1+\frac{1}{2}$
	>$\implies \begin{cases}a<M'&N=M'-1\\M\leq a<M+1&N=M'\\M+1\leq a&N=M'+1\end{cases}$
	>证明二：反证法
	>$假设\forall a\geq0,~a<N\lor a\geq N+1$
	>$\implies \begin{cases}a<0\lor 1\leq a\implies a\geq 1&N=0\\a\geq n+1&N=n\\a<n+1\lor n+1+1\leq a\implies a\geq n+2&N=n+1\end{cases}\implies \forall n\in N,~a\geq n 矛盾$
	>$负数同理$
	>唯一性：
	>$\exists N<N',~N\leq x<N+1,~N'\leq x<N'+1\implies x<N+1\leq N'\leq x矛盾$
	- 任意两个实数之间必存在一个有理数$$\forall a,~b\in R,~\exists N\in Q,~a<N<b$$
	>$\exists N_{0},~N_{0}(b-a)>1,~\exists n,~n\leq N_{0}a<n+1<N_{0}a+1<N_{0}b$
	>$\implies \exists N=\frac{n+1}{N_{0}},~a<N<b$

# 3 实数组合：实数标准拓扑
- ==定义== **实数子集的最值**：
	定义实数子集$E$的最值$$max(E)=M\Leftrightarrow M\in E\land \forall a\in E,~a\leq M$$$$min(E)=N\Leftrightarrow N\in E\land \forall a\in E,~a\geq M$$

- ==定理== 实数子集的**最值性质**：
	- $$\forall x \in E,~min(E)\leq x\leq max(E)当且仅当E=\{ x \}时min(E)=max(E)=x$$
	>$略$

- ==定义== 实数的绝对值：
	- 定义实数$x$的绝对值$$|~x~|=\begin{cases}x&x>0\\0&x=0\\-x&x<0\end{cases}$$
		- 非退化性$$\forall x \in R,~|~x~|\geq 0当且仅当x=0等号成立$$
		- 齐次性$$\forall a\in R,~|~ax~|=|~a~||~x~|$$
		- 三角不等式$$|~|~a~|-|~b~|~|\leq |~a\pm b~|\leq |~a~|+|~b~|$$
		>$分类讨论即可$

- ==定理== 实数的绝对值性质：
	- $max(a,~b)=\frac{a+b}{2}+\frac{|~a-b~|}{2},~min(a,~b)=\frac{a+b}{2}-\frac{|~a-b~|}{2}$
	>$验证即可$

- ==定义== **实数子集的界**：实数最值替代
	- 定义实数子集的界，称$M$是相应$E$的界$$E有界\Leftrightarrow \exists M>0,~\forall x \in E,~|~x~|\leq M~~~~~E无界\Leftrightarrow\forall M>0,~\exists x_{0}\in E,~|~x~|>M$$$$E有上界\Leftrightarrow\exists M>0,~\forall x \in E,~x\leq M~~~~~E无上界\Leftrightarrow\forall M>0,~\exists x_{0}\in E,~x>M$$$$E有下界\Leftrightarrow \exists M<0,~\forall x \in E,~x\leq M~~~~~E无下界\Leftrightarrow\forall M<0,~\exists x_{0}\in E,~x<M$$
	- 定义实数子集$E$的确界$M$$$M是E的上确界，sup(E)=M\Leftrightarrow M是E的最小上界$$$$M是E的下确界,~inf(E)=M\Leftrightarrow M是E的最大下界$$若$E$无上界，记$sup(E)=+\infty$，若$E$无下界，记$inf(E)=-\infty$
		- 确界等价条件$$sup(E)=M\Leftrightarrow\forall x \in E,~x\leq M\land \forall\varepsilon>0,~\exists x_{0}\in E,~x_{0}>M-\varepsilon$$$$inf(E)=M\Leftrightarrow\forall x \in E,~x\geq M\land \forall\varepsilon>0,~\exists x_{0}\in E,~x_{0}<M+\varepsilon$$
		>$\forall M',~\forall x \in E,~x\leq M',~M-\varepsilon<x_{0}\leq M'\implies \forall\varepsilon>0,~M<M'+\varepsilon\implies M\leq M'$
		>$\exists\varepsilon_{0},~\forall x \in E,~x\leq M-\varepsilon_{0}\implies \exists M'=M-\varepsilon_{0},~M'<M$

- ==定理== **确界存在性定理**：**实数基本定理I**
	- 实数子集有上界必有唯一上确界，有下界必有唯一下确界
	>$设实数子集E有上界M,~x_{0}\in E,~n\in N^{+}$
	>$\exists K\in N,~ \frac{K}{n}\geq M,~\exists L\in N,~ \frac{L}{n}\leq x_{0}\implies \exists m_{n},~L\leq m_{n}<K\land\text{！}\frac{m_{n}}{n}不是E的上界但 \frac{m_{n}+1}{n}是E的上界$
	>设$\forall N'\in N^{+},~\forall n,~n'\geq N',~\begin{cases} \frac{m_{n}+1}{n}> \frac{m_{n'}}{n'}& \frac{m_{n}}{n}- \frac{m_{n'}}{n'}>-\frac{1}{n'}\geq-\frac{1}{N'}\\ \frac{m_{n'}+1}{n'}> \frac{m_{n}}{n}& \frac{m_{n}}{n}- \frac{m_{n'}}{n'}<\frac{1}{n'}\leq \frac{1}{N'}\end{cases}\implies \forall N',~\forall n,~n'\geq N',~|~ \frac{m_{n}}{n} - \frac{m_{n'}}{n'}~|\leq \frac{1}{N'}\implies\text{！} \frac{m_{n}}{n}是柯西序列$
	>$定义实数\text{！}S=LIM\left(  \frac{m_{n}}{n} \right)=LIM\left(  \frac{m_{n}+1}{n} \right)$
	>$\forall x \in E,~x\leq \frac{m_{n}+1}{n}\implies x\leq S$
	>$\forall y是E的上界,~y\geq \frac{m_{n}}{n}\implies y\geq S$
	>$唯一性$

- ==定理== 实数子集的**界性质**：实数子集的确界计算
	- $inf(E)\leq sup(E)当且仅当E=\{ x \}时inf(E)=sup(E)=x$
	>$\forall a\in E,~inf(E)\leq a\leq sup(E)$
	- $M=sup(E)\in E\Leftrightarrow M=max(E),~N=inf(E)\in E\Leftrightarrow N=min(E)$
	>$sup(E)\in E\implies \forall x \in E,~x\leq sup(E)\implies max(E)=sup(E)$
	>$max(E)=M\implies \forall x \in E,~x\leq M\land\forall\varepsilon>0,~\exists M\in E,~M>M-\varepsilon\implies max(E)=M=sup(E)$
	- $$\forall x \in A,~y\in B,~x\leq y\implies sup(A)\leq inf(B)$$
	>$\forall y\in B,~\forall x \in A,~x\leq y\implies y是A的上界$
	- 令$E=A\cup B$$$sup(E)=max\{ sup(A),~sup(B) \}$$$$inf(E)=min\{ inf(A),~inf(B) \}$$
	>$\forall x \in E\implies x \in A\lor x \in B\implies x\leq supA\lor x\leq supB\implies x\leq max(sup(A),~sup(B))\implies sup(E)\leq max(sup(A),~sup(B))$
	>$\forall x \in A,~x \in E\implies x\leq sup(E)\implies sup(A)\leq sup(E),~同理sup(B)\leq sup(E)\implies max(sup(A),~sup(B))\leq sup(E)$
	- 定义$-E=\{ x~|~-x\in E \}$$$inf(-E)= -sup(E)$$$$sup(-E)= -inf(E)$$
	>$\forall x \in E,~x\leq sup(E)\implies-x\geq -sup(E)\implies inf(-E)\geq-sup(E)$
	>$\forall -x \in-E,~-x\geq inf(-E)\implies x\leq-inf(-E)\implies sup(E)\leq-inf(-E)\implies-sup(E)\geq inf(-E)$
	- 定义$A+B=\{ z~|~z=x+y,~x \in A,~y\in B \}$$$sup(A+B)=sup(A)+sup(B)$$$$inf(A+B)=inf(A)+inf(B)$$
	>$\forall x \in A,~x\leq supA,~\forall y \in B,~y\leq supB\implies \forall z=x+y\in A+B,~z\leq sup(A)+sup(B)\implies sup(A+B)\leq sup(A)+sup(B)$
	>$\forall \varepsilon>0,~\exists x \in A,~x\geq sup(A)-\varepsilon,~\exists y \in B,~y\geq sup(B)-\varepsilon\implies \exists z=x+y\geq sup(A)+sup(B)-2\varepsilon\implies sup(A+B)\geq sup(A)+sup(B)$
